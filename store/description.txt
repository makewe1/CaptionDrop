Your video. Your subtitles.

CaptionDrop lets you add your own SRT subtitle files to supported online videos. Select a detected video, choose a subtitle file from your computer, and keep the focus on what you are watching.

MAKE CAPTIONS EASY TO READ
- Choose the text size, text color, and a black or transparent background.
- Drag subtitles to your preferred position within the video.
- Show or hide captions, or replace the subtitle file from the player controls.

FOLLOW YOUR PLAYBACK
Captions stay synchronized when you play, pause, seek, or change playback speed. Subtitle timing comes from your SRT file.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
CaptionDrop remembers subtitle files for individual videos on your device. Enable automatic loading for a website to restore saved captions when you revisit the same video. Your chosen appearance and position are remembered too. You can forget saved subtitles from the player settings.

KEEP YOUR FILES LOCAL
No account is required, and your subtitle files are not uploaded. CaptionDrop processes and stores your captions locally in your browser. You choose which websites receive access for automatic loading.

GET STARTED
1. Open a webpage with a video and click CaptionDrop.
2. Select the video and choose your local .srt file.
3. Use the CaptionDrop button on the player to adjust your captions.

CaptionDrop supports accessible HTML5 video players. Compatibility varies by website; some embedded, custom, or browser-restricted players may not be accessible. This version loads existing SRT files and does not generate or translate captions.
